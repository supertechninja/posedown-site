Common questions

Where are my photos? Shots stay on this device. Backup is off until you turn it on in Settings — iCloud on iPhone, Android Backup on Android. After you change the Android switch, restart Posedown.

Why didn’t my photos show up in Photos or Gallery? Camera shots stay in Posedown unless you turn on Save camera shots in Settings (Photos on iPhone, Gallery on Android). Imports are not copied back out.

How do I share without a watermark? Watch a rewarded ad from the share sheet. Timeline reels work the same way on both platforms.
