Common questions

Where are my photos? Shots stay on this iPhone. iCloud backup is off until you turn it on in Settings.

How do I restore Posedown Pro? Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple Account that bought it.

How do I lock a shot? Open the shot and move it to Locked. Face ID or your passcode is required to open Locked.
