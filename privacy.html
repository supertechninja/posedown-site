Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026. Posedown is made by Joshua McWilliams.

What Posedown stores

Posing photos, notes, check-ins, and optional bodyweight stay on your iPhone. The app does not require an account.

  • Camera and photo library. Used to capture poses and to import stills when you choose. Saving camera shots to the Photos app is off unless you turn it on in Settings.
  • iCloud. Off by default. If you turn on backup, shots can sync through your iCloud account and Apple’s CloudKit.

Advertising

Sharing without a watermark, including timeline reels, may show a rewarded ad from Google Mobile Ads. That SDK may collect device identifiers, IP address, crash and performance data, and ad interaction data as described in Google’s policies.

What we do not do

We do not sell your posing photos. We do not run our own analytics backend. Support email you send to jtmacnb@gmail.com is kept only to answer you.
