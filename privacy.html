Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026. Posedown is made by Joshua McWilliams. This one policy covers the iPhone app and the Android app. Store listings both link here.

What Posedown stores

Posing photos, notes, check-ins, and optional bodyweight stay on your device. There is no Posedown account and no Posedown server for your catalog.

  • Camera. Used when you shoot a pose in the app.
  • System library. Used when you import stills. Saving camera shots out of Posedown is off unless you turn it on in Settings — to Photos on iPhone, or Gallery on Android.

Backup

Off by default on both platforms. Until you turn it on, posing photos are not included in device backup.

  • iPhone. Turning backup on can sync the catalog through iCloud / CloudKit with your Apple ID.
  • Android. Turning backup on includes the catalog in Android Backup (your Google account’s device backup). Restart Posedown after you change this so photos move into or out of the backup folder.

Face blur (Android)

Optional, off by default. Face detection runs on-device with ML Kit and does not upload your photos for that purpose. The iPhone app does not include this control in the first release.

Advertising

Sharing without a watermark, including timeline reels, may show a rewarded ad from Google Mobile Ads. That SDK may collect advertising identifiers (Advertising ID on Android; Apple’s advertising identifier on iPhone when available), IP address, crash and performance data, and ad interaction data as described in Google’s policies.

On Android in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland a consent prompt may appear before ads are requested.

What we do not do

We do not sell your posing photos. We do not run our own analytics backend. Support email you send to jtmacnb@gmail.com is kept only to answer you.
