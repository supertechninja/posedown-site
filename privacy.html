Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026. Posedown is made by Joshua McWilliams.

What Posedown stores

Posing photos, notes, check-ins, and optional bodyweight stay on your iPhone. The app does not require an account.

  • Camera and photo library. Used to capture poses and to import or export stills when you choose.
  • Face ID. Used only on device to open Locked and, if you enable it, to unlock the app. Biometric data never leaves the phone.
  • iCloud. Off by default. If you turn on backup, shots can sync through your iCloud account and Apple’s CloudKit.

Purchases

Posedown Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple. Apple handles payment details. The app stores whether Pro is unlocked on this device and checks that entitlement with StoreKit.

Advertising

Free timeline reel exports may show a rewarded ad from Google Mobile Ads. That SDK may collect device identifiers, IP address, crash and performance data, and ad interaction data as described in Google’s policies. Pro removes that ad.

What we do not do

We do not sell your posing photos. We do not run our own analytics backend. Support email you send to jtmacnb@gmail.com is kept only to answer you.
