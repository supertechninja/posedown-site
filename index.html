The posing journal

Stage every pose.

Track every week. Hit it better next time. Posedown files every shot under the pose it belongs to — men’s and women’s packs, check-ins, compare, and a timeline of one mandatory across the season.

Shoot in the app or import from Photos (iPhone) or Gallery (Android). Line up with an optional silhouette in the camera. Compare last time to today. Stills and reels carry a Posedown watermark unless you watch a rewarded ad to remove it.
